How this estimate is calculated

Fixed charge: 11 local currency units. Service and platform fees: 5% each of that fixed charge. Where a tax rate is shown, it is applied to the 12.10-unit fee subtotal as a planning proxy; a published standard rate may not apply to this transaction. An assumed gateway rate is grossed up against the fee subtotal plus tax, then rounded up to the currency's minor-unit precision.

The India gateway planning assumption is 2.36% effective; the generic international planning assumption is 3.54%. These are not country-specific provider quotes and do not confirm payment support. Real gateway fees can depend on merchant location, payment method, currency support, FX and contract terms.

No exchange-rate conversion is applied: this estimate uses 11 units of each local currency, not the current equivalent of ₹11. The 12.10-unit fee subtotal is before tax, gateway recovery and business operating expenses, so this estimate cannot guarantee net profit.

Payment status and limits Booking fee totals on this page are estimates, not final payable checkout totals. A listed local currency does not confirm payment-method or settlement support in that country. Paid T2I packages remain disabled until a secure server-side payment and token-credit flow is implemented and verified. Booking-fee estimates must be validated against applicable local rules and the payment provider before use.
