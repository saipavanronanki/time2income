2. Information the Service Handles

Depending on how you use TIME2INCOME, the service may handle the following information:

2.1 Account Information

  • Email address handled by Supabase Authentication.
  • Profile name.
  • Country and city.
  • Account role.
  • Language preferences.
  • Currency preferences.

2.2 Profile Photos

Users provide a professional head-and-shoulders photo for their account.

  • Photos are stored in private Supabase Storage.
  • Signed-in marketplace members may view them where account profiles appear, including eligible provider listings and booking or offer activity.
  • The browser resizes and re-encodes profile photos, removing embedded image metadata.

A profile photo is not identity verification and does not establish a person's legal identity.

2.3 Private Professional Profiles

Signed-in users may create multiple professional profiles for purposes such as:

  • Job seeking.
  • Freelancing.
  • Professional services.
  • Businesses.
  • Entrepreneurship.
  • Investing.
  • Study.
  • Creative work.

Profile details are stored in the user's account metadata.

Optional profile photos and uploaded PDF, Word, PowerPoint, PNG, JPG, JPEG or WebP files are stored in private Supabase Storage buckets accessible only to the relevant account.

These professional profiles are not included in provider search and are not shared with other users.

Uploaded originals remain unchanged. Profile exports and downloaded copies of uploaded PDFs include a "time2income.com | Powered by Spactisu" footer.

2.4 Provider Profiles

Provider profiles may include:

  • Skills and service categories.
  • Biography and professional information.
  • Work preferences.
  • Service city and country.
  • Rates and pricing information.
  • Languages.
  • Portfolio images.

Published provider profiles are available to signed-in marketplace users.

2.5 Signup Identity Documents

New accounts must confirm their email address and sign in before submitting a photo or PDF of a valid identity document showing the user's photograph and ID number.

  • Users may select a file or take a photo using a compatible device camera.
  • The original file is stored unchanged in a private Supabase Storage bucket for authorized review only.
  • Embedded photo metadata may remain in the original document.
  • The document and ID number are not displayed on marketplace profiles.
  • While email confirmation is pending, the selected file may be temporarily staged in browser storage and removed after upload or local staging cleanup. The exact staging window must be confirmed before publication.
  • No final retention period for identity documents has been approved. The platform should prefer an authorized KYC provider and avoid retaining duplicate documents where possible.
  • The ID number is not separately extracted into account metadata.

Document submission does not automatically mean that an identity has been approved or verified.

Review outcomes do not determine account access. After approval, an identity-reviewed badge or status may appear on marketplace listings. Verified profiles may receive ranking priority over unverified profiles.

Where mutual reviews are enabled, weighted reviews for either party may influence provider, offer and task ordering within marketplace results. Until that feature is enabled, task posts use recency within verification groups.

2.6 Optional Provider Selfie Review

Providers may optionally submit a selfie for private review.

  • The browser resizes and re-encodes the image, removing embedded image metadata.
  • The selfie is stored in a separate private Supabase Storage bucket.
  • It is not displayed on the public provider profile.

A selfie alone does not establish a person's legal identity.

2.7 Optional Provider Location

Providers may choose the "Set approximate location" option.

  • The browser rounds coordinates to one decimal place before saving them with the provider profile.
  • These coarse coordinates are used to rank nearby providers.
  • The approximate coordinates are visible to signed-in marketplace users.
  • Providers can remove this information from their profile.

2.8 Search Location

Visitors may grant browser location permission to rank providers by distance.

  • Search coordinates remain in page memory for the current session.
  • The website does not write these search coordinates to the user's account or browser storage.
  • Manually entering a city remains available as an alternative.

2.9 Marketplace Activity

The service may handle:

  • Tasks and task descriptions.
  • Offers and offer statuses.
  • Booking requests and booking statuses.
  • Booking messages.
  • Reviews submitted after completed bookings.

Marketplace rules may change whether a task listing is visible or can be removed. Listing expiry or removal does not determine retention of related bookings, messages, transaction records, backups, or audit data, the schedule for those records remains pending legal review.

2.10 Browser Preferences

The website stores selected language, currency and manually entered marketplace city preferences in browser storage.

2.11 Task-Action Payments

When enabled, Razorpay processes applicable fees for:

  • Task posts after the first free post.
  • Task-detail openings after the first free opening.

Razorpay handles checkout payment details. The website sends payment and order references, along with task-action records, to Supabase for verification.

TIME2INCOME does not currently process payments for service bookings.

  • Operate provider profiles and professional profiles.
  • Enable task and service discovery.
  • Support offers and booking requests.
  • Display reviews and relevant marketplace information.
  • Rank nearby providers when location features are enabled.
  • Remember browser preferences.
  • Review submitted identity documents.
  • Verify task-action payments when checkout is available.
  • Respond to customer support and privacy requests.
  • Maintain platform security and prevent misuse.

Information should only be used for purposes consistent with applicable privacy requirements and the actual features of the deployed service.

5. Data Retention

TIME2INCOME has not finalized retention periods or deletion deadlines for identity documents and KYC records, payment evidence and transaction records, user accounts and profiles, task and booking records, or chat and messaging data. No fixed schedule should be inferred from this draft. Existing listing expiry or local-file cleanup behavior is separate from the retention of related records, backups, messages, and audit data.

The final schedule and deletion procedure are pending legal review. Where legally permitted, eligible personal information should be securely deleted or anonymized. Records must be preserved where required by law or a valid legal hold applies, and the reason for delayed or denied deletion should be explained to the user.

A policy-driven retention scheduler and account-deletion workflow are not available. The signed-in account form and support database provide intake-only request tracking with a ticket ID and visible status. Submitting a request does not automatically delete or change account data; a person must review it, status updates require an authorized staff process that is not yet available in the app, and legally required records or valid legal holds may affect processing. 6. Security and Access

TIME2INCOME uses private storage for sensitive uploaded documents and account-restricted files.

Access to identity documents and optional provider selfies must be limited to specifically authorized KYC administrators and compliance personnel. Customer support should see verification status only by default, developers and general IT staff must not have routine access. Sensitive reads and deletion decisions must be audit-logged. Where an authorized KYC provider can verify a document, TIME2INCOME should store the provider reference and verification status rather than duplicate the document.

The platform should maintain appropriate technical and organizational safeguards, including:

  • Access controls.
  • Secure authentication.
  • Private storage permissions.
  • Restricted administrative access.
  • Appropriate handling of security incidents.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.
