1. Getting Started

How do I join or sign in?

Use the Join or Sign in options on TIME2INCOME. If email confirmation is requested, verify your email address before signing in. Account registration and login availability may depend on the current platform policy.

Is TIME2INCOME free to join?

Yes. Creating an account and exploring the marketplace are free. Some task-related features may have fees, which are displayed before payment.

Can I use TIME2INCOME on a computer?

You can browse the website on supported devices. Account registration and login availability depend on the current platform policy.

Which countries does TIME2INCOME support?

TIME2INCOME is designed for a global marketplace. Available services, features and currencies may vary by country.

2. Profiles & Verification

How do I appear in provider search?

Sign in, open My Profile, complete your provider details and publish your profile. Approximate location is optional. If enabled, the website rounds location data before saving it to support nearby-first search.

Can I be both a service provider and a customer?

Yes. You can request services and offer services using the same account.

Is identity verification mandatory?

Verification requirements may vary depending on the service, country and platform rules. A profile should only display a verified status after successful verification.

Can I hide or update my profile?

Use your profile settings to update your information and manage visibility where those controls are available.

3. Finding Work & Services

How do I find a service provider?

Browse service categories, search for providers and use available location or other filters to find suitable professionals.

Can I offer remote services?

Yes, where the selected service supports remote delivery. Some services require in-person availability.

How can I find work?

Browse available tasks and opportunities, review their requirements and submit an offer where the feature is available.

4. Tasks & Bookings

How do I request a service?

Browse providers and send a booking request. A request is not confirmed until the provider accepts it. Agree on the work, schedule, location and price in advance.

What happens after I send a booking request?

The provider must review and accept the request. Until acceptance, the booking remains unconfirmed.

Can I cancel a booking request?

Pending requests may be withdrawn where the platform supports this option. Accepted bookings are subject to the applicable cancellation policy.

What happens if a provider does not respond?

A pending request is not a confirmed booking. Contact support if you need assistance with an unresolved request.

Can I edit or delete a task?

An open task can be edited once or deleted by its owner, subject to the platform's task rules. A task is deleted when its booking is confirmed complete.

How long does an open task remain active?

Open tasks remain active for up to five days, subject to the platform's task rules.

Can the customer and provider review each other?

After a booking is completed, both the customer and provider can each leave one review for the other. Reviews help highlight trusted profiles and customer tasks within their area.

5. Payments, Fees & Currency

Does TIME2INCOME process service-booking payments?

No. TIME2INCOME does not currently collect or release payments for service bookings. Customers and providers should agree on payment arrangements directly.

Are there any task-related fees?

Creating an account and exploring tasks are free. Each task post and first opening of a task uses 1 T2I token; reopening a task you already unlocked is free. Eligible accounts receive a one-time 100 T2I welcome reward. T2I token purchases are not available yet.

Do I have to pay again to reopen the same task?

No. Reopening the same unlocked task is free.

Which currency will I see?

Your displayed currency depends on your selected country and supported account settings. Always check the checkout amount and currency before confirming payment.

Are task fees refundable?

Refund eligibility depends on the applicable refund policy and transaction circumstances. Contact support for assistance with a payment issue.

What is TIME2INCOME Token (T2I)?

If T2I credits are activated, their purpose, usage, conversion rules and restrictions will be explained in the applicable platform terms. Do not assume that T2I credits are equivalent to cash.
