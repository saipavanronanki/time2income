        HELP & SUPPORT

        Frequently asked questions

        Is TIME2INCOME free to use?

        Creating an account and browsing are free. Online payments are not available yet, so agree on the price and payment method directly with the other person.

        How can I find work?

        Create a provider profile or send an offer on an open task. Customers can also send you a booking request.

        Is income guaranteed?

        No. Earnings depend on your skills, availability and demand. The calculator and finder provide estimates and ideas only.

        How can I stay safe?

        Agree on the details in the booking chat. Never share passwords, verification codes or bank details. Avoid large upfront payments and meet in a public place for a first in-person meeting.

        Who can leave a review?

        The customer can leave one review after a booking is completed.
