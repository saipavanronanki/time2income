        HELP & SUPPORT

        Frequently asked questions

        Is TIME2INCOME free to use?

        Creating an account and exploring tasks are free. Each task post and first opening of a task costs 1 T2I. Reopening a task you already unlocked is free. Eligible accounts receive a one-time 100 T2I welcome reward; token purchases are not available yet.

        How can I find work?

        Create a provider profile or send an offer on an open task. Customers can also send you a booking request.

        Is income guaranteed?

        No. Earnings depend on your skills, availability and demand. The calculator and finder provide estimates and ideas only.

        How can I stay safe?

        Agree on the details in the booking chat. Never share passwords, verification codes or bank details. Avoid large upfront payments and meet in a public place for a first in-person meeting.

        Who can leave a review?

        After a booking is completed, both the customer and provider can each leave one review for the other. Reviews help highlight trusted profiles and customer tasks within their area.
